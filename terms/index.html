4a. Sheety List — Google API data use

Sheety List uses Google Sign-In and requests access to your Google account via two OAuth scopes:

  • Google Drive (drive.file): This scope allows the app to create and manage a single file in your Google Drive — a spreadsheet named "Sheety List." This scope is limited to files the app itself creates. Sheety List cannot see, access, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets): This scope allows the app to read from and write to the "Sheety List" spreadsheet. This is how your tasks are loaded when you open the app and saved when you add or update them.

What data is accessed: The app accesses only the contents of the "Sheety List" spreadsheet — your task titles, categories, priorities, due dates, and notes. No other Google account data is accessed.

How data is used: Your task data is read to display your list in the app and written when you add, edit, or complete tasks. It is used solely to power the Sheety List task management experience.

Storage: Your task data lives entirely in your own Google Drive — in a spreadsheet named "Sheety List" that the app creates on first use. Sheety Tools does not copy, store, or transmit your data to any server, database, or third party. We have no backend. The app runs entirely in your browser and communicates directly with Google's APIs on your behalf.

Sharing: Your data is never shared with, sold to, or accessible by anyone other than you. Sheety Tools has no access to your spreadsheet or its contents.

Retention: Your data remains in your Google Drive until you delete it. Revoking the app's access (via your Google account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions) immediately removes the app's ability to access your spreadsheet. The spreadsheet itself remains in your Drive and can be deleted manually at any time.

Sheety List's use of Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
