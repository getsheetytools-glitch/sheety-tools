    Focus Budget

    Quick tour
    Step 1 of 2

    A visual way to spend your attention on purpose.

    Your focus is limited. This tool makes the tradeoffs visible — so the “top slice” is honest, and everything else is a deliberate choice.

    What this is

    A calm thinking tool for priorities — especially when everything feels important.

    What this isn’t

    Not a task manager. Not a time tracker. Not a productivity system.
    It’s allowed to change.

    Mental model: your attention is a pie. Bigger slices get more focus. Smaller slices are tradeoffs.

    How it works

    1) Add focus items

    Anything that competes for your attention counts: tasks, projects, people, worries, goals.

    2) Rank them

    Drag items (or use arrows). Slice size follows rank. Moving one up always costs something else.

    3) See the cost

    Moving one thing up always pushes something else down.

    The pie makes that cost visible — without judgement.
