About

I'm an instructional designer with a straightforward obsession: making learning less painful — not just in a general sense, but specifically where it hurts most, where people are expected to absorb critical information fast and without much help.

I've spent my career architecting learning experiences, and Sheety Tools is where that work takes a more direct form. Sheety Onboarding started because employee onboarding is one of the biggest, most neglected pain points in that space — and most existing tools are either bloated, ugly, or both.

A note on how this was built: I'm not a coder. The development here leaned on AI tools — Claude and ChatGPT among them. But the ideas, the product decisions, the design thinking — those belong to me. I think that's an honest way to build in 2026, and I'd rather say so plainly than pretend otherwise.
